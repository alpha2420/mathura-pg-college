Financial Aid

Scholarships & Financial Support

Multiple scholarship schemes are available for eligible students to make quality education accessible to all.

📜

UP Government Scholarships

Students from economically weaker sections, SC/ST/OBC communities can apply for state government scholarships through the UP Scholarship portal.

🎓

Merit-Based Scholarships

Students with exceptional academic performance may be eligible for merit scholarships. Contact the college office for details and eligibility criteria.

💼

Minority Scholarships

Minority community students can avail scholarships offered by the central and state government. Applications must be submitted as per the deadline.

  • 📰 Daily newspapers and academic journals
