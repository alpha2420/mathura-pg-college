Admissions

Admission Process & Eligibility

🎓

UG (B.A.) Eligibility

  • Passed Intermediate (10+2) or equivalent examination
  • From a recognised Board (UP Board / CBSE / ICSE etc.)
  • No minimum percentage required (merit-based selection)
  • Must upload Intermediate marksheet / result during application
  • Original documents required at time of admission
Apply for UG →
📜

PG (M.A.) Eligibility

  • Graduation (B.A. / B.Sc. / B.Com) from a recognised university
  • Minimum 45% marks in relevant subject at UG level
  • Subject-wise eligibility applies — check the subject you wish to pursue
  • JNCU university guidelines apply for PG admissions
  • Documents: Degree / Provisional certificate, Marksheets, Transfer Certificate
Apply for PG →

Important Notice for UG Applicants

Students who applied for UG Sem-I admission but left their Intermediate result as "Result Awaiting" must immediately log in to the student portal and upload their Intermediate result. Failure to do so may result in the college not being responsible for their admission status.

Conduct

General Rules

  • 01. Students must maintain regular attendance as per university norms (minimum 75%).
  • 02. All students are required to carry their identity card within college premises.
  • 03. Use of mobile phones in classrooms and library is strictly prohibited.
  • 04. Students must dress appropriately and maintain the decorum of the institution.
  • 05. Damage to college property will result in disciplinary action and financial liability.
  • 06. All disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of Ballia courts only.
Safety

Anti-Ragging Policy

🚫
Zero Tolerance for Ragging

Ragging in any form — physical, verbal, mental or online — is strictly prohibited on and off campus. Any student found guilty of ragging will be expelled from the institution.

  • ✓ An Anti-Ragging Committee is constituted every academic year.
  • ✓ All students must sign an anti-ragging undertaking at the time of admission.
  • ✓ Complaints can be reported to the Principal's office directly and in confidence.
  • ✓ JNCU and state government anti-ragging guidelines are strictly followed.
