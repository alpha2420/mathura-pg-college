About Mathura P.G. College

A legacy of academic excellence and value-based education in Rasra, Ballia, Uttar Pradesh.

Our Story

A History of
Academic Excellence

Mathura P.G. College was established with a singular vision: to bring quality higher education to the people of Rasra and the surrounding regions of Ballia district in eastern Uttar Pradesh. Over the decades, the college has steadily grown into one of the most trusted degree colleges in the region.

Affiliated to Jannayak Chandra Shekhar University (JNCU), Ballia, the college offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in arts and humanities. Its alumni have gone on to serve in distinguished roles across government, education, social service, and other fields, making the college truly proud.

The institution stands as an alma mater which all alumni look back on with fond memories — a place where knowledge meets character, and where every student is given the opportunity to discover their potential.

Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Core Values

The principles that guide every decision we make and every programme we deliver.

🔭

Vision

To be a leading centre of higher learning that empowers students with knowledge, values, and life skills — enabling them to contribute meaningfully to society and the nation.

🎯

Mission

To provide accessible, quality education through a balanced mix of academic rigour, character building, and practical exposure — fostering critical thinking and civic responsibility in every student.

💡

Core Values

Excellence in education · Integrity in conduct · Inclusivity in access · Respect for diversity · Commitment to community. These values guide every teacher, student, and staff member.
