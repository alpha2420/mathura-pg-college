Terms, Conditions & Policies

General institutional rules, admissions guidelines, and code of conduct for students and visitors.

1. General Rules & Regulations

All students enrolled in Mathura P.G. College, Rasra, Ballia are subject to the discipline and regulations of Jannayak Chandra Shekhar University (JNCU) and the college administration.

  • Minimum 75% attendance in lectures and practicals is compulsory to appear in university examinations.
  • Possession or consumption of tobacco, alcohol, or illicit substances on campus is strictly prohibited.
  • Students must carry their valid College Identity Card at all times on the college premises.

2. Anti-Ragging Policy

Ragging in any form is completely prohibited inside and outside the college campus as per Supreme Court of India directives and UGC regulations. Any student found guilty of ragging will face immediate suspension, rustication, and legal action.

3. Online Admission & Fee Payments

Admissions are granted based on merit and university eligibility criteria. All online fee transactions are processed through secure payment gateways. Fees once paid are non-refundable except in cases explicitly permitted by University guidelines.

4. Privacy Policy

Mathura P.G. College respects your privacy. Information collected during online registration, admissions, or enquiry is used solely for academic administration, university enrollment, and official communications.
