Discipline

Proctorial Board

The Proctorial Board is responsible for maintaining discipline and order within the college campus. It ensures that all students follow the code of conduct and that the college environment remains safe, respectful, and conducive to learning.

  • •Oversees student discipline and campus conduct
  • •Reviews and resolves student complaints and grievances
  • •Implements anti-ragging and safety measures
  • •Coordinates with the principal on disciplinary proceedings
